# GRAISIX Technologies website

This is a static website prepared for GitHub Pages. Keep `index.html` and the `assets`, `css`, `js`, and `projects` folders together at the publishing root.

## Before launch

- Confirm that `https://graisix.com/` is your domain and update canonical/social URLs if needed.
- The `hello@graisix.com` mailbox is planned, not confirmed active. Create and test it, then update the status text in `js/site-config.js`.
- The homepage contact form points to Formspree form ID `mkjgqjzr`. Sign in to your Formspree account and confirm the form belongs to you and delivers to the intended inbox. The website has not submitted a live test.
- The privacy, terms, cookies, and refund pages are drafts and marked `noindex`. Review their content against your actual practices and applicable law before removing the draft notices or allowing search engines to index them. Do not insert invented company or legal details.
- The public brand is identified as an unregistered project. Do not describe it as an incorporated company unless that changes.

## Website files

- `index.html`: homepage
- `projects/`: five project pages
- `css/` and `js/`: website styles and behavior
- `assets/logo/favicon.svg`: browser icon
- `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`: search-engine discovery files; draft policy pages are intentionally left out of the sitemap
